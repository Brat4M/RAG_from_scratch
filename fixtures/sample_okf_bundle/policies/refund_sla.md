# Refund Service Level Agreement Policy

Customer support and billing dispute guidelines.

### Resolution Tiers
1. Standard Refunds: Requests within 14 calendar days of order creation in [Customer Orders](/tables/orders.md) are auto-approved if order status is `COMPLETED`.
2. High-Risk Customer Escalation: If customer dispute risk score in [Customer Profiles](/tables/customers.md) exceeds 0.70, manager review is mandatory.
3. Accounting Impact: Approved refunds trigger reversal entries updating [Gross Margin](/metrics/gross_margin.md).
